## 🌌 The Origin Story
For centuries, humanity has played the same foundational board games: Chess (6th century), Ludo/Pachisi (6th century), Go (2500+ years ago). While video games evolved into hyper-engaging visual experiences, tabletop grid strategy remained largely unchanged.

**AURA SPHERES** was engineered with AI to invent **a brand-new tactical sport**:
- **Zero boring dice rolls:** Every roll has a distinct tactical impact (Moves, Swaps, Shockwaves, or Shields).
- **Zero setup time:** Instant browser access on mobile, tablet, and PC.
- **Gen Z Aesthetics:** Holographic polyhedral shards, rotating energy orbit rings, levitation physics, and synthesized cosmic audio.

## ⚡ Core Features

### 👥 2 to 4 Players Multi-Faction
| Faction | Color | Sigil | Archetype |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Player 1** | Sapphire Azure | `◈` | Aero Diamond Matrix |
| **Player 2** | Ruby Radiant | `⚡` | Plasma Arc Shard |
| **Player 3** | Emerald Zenith | `⬡` | Quantum Hex Core |
| **Player 4** | Obsidian Gold | `▲` | Apex Radiant Crest |

### 🎲 The Quantum Catalyst (Personal Side Dice)
No passing a single dice around. Each player has their own illuminated catalyst widget:
- **`1, 2, 3 Steps`** — Directional orthogonal pathing across the 7×7 Lumina Grid.
- **`🔄 SWAP`** — Dimensional transversion: Instantly swap coordinates with any enemy piece.
- **`⚡ PULSE`** — Radial shockwave: Knocks all adjacent opponent pieces back 2 tiles.
- **`🛡️ SHIELD`** — Team invulnerability barrier: Immunizes your pieces against captures and swaps for 1 round.

### 🎨 In-Game Skin Selector (Gen Z Aesthetics)
Switch between 4 high-tech piece styles on the fly:
1. **💎 Cyber Prisms** *(Default)* — Valorant-style faceted crystals with floating orbit rings.
2. **⚡ Neon Runes** — Sharp cyber-rhombus diamond geometries.
3. **🛡️ Mecha Crests** — Sci-Fi Aegis energy shield totems.
4. **🌌 Astral Glyphs** — Celestial octagram star matrices.

### ☀️ The Solar Singularity (Center Goal)
At coordinate `(3,3)` lies the cosmic core:
- Featuring dual counter-rotating starlight rings and an animated event-horizon eye.
- **Victory Condition:** First faction to guide **2 pieces into the Solar Singularity** absorbs the light and wins!

### 🌌 Eclipse Banishment & Starlight Rescue
- Landing directly on an opponent piece triggers an **Eclipse**: the piece is banished to *Starlight Orbit*.
- Players can spend a turn to **Rescue** a banished orb back to their sanctuary corner.

### ✦ Tactical Aura Cards
Landing on the 4 golden rune altars unlocks powerful one-time catalysts:
- **⏳ Time Warp:** Extra roll immediately this turn.
- **🌌 Gravity Surge:** Pulls all your active pieces 1 step closer to the Solar Singularity.
- **✨ Aegis Barrier:** Grants team-wide invulnerability shield for one full round.

### 🤖 3 Game Modes
1. **VS Oracle AI:** 1 Human player vs 1 to 3 intelligent algorithmic bots.
2. **Pass & Play (Local Multiplayer):** 2 to 4 human players sharing the same screen.
3. **🌌 Watch AI Battle (Spectator Mode):** Automated battle royale between AI factions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Structure:** Pure Semantic HTML5.
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS3 with GPU-accelerated CSS Grid, Glassmorphism, dynamic polygon clip-paths, and radial glowing backdrops.
- **Logic:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ modular state machine).
- **Audio:** Web Audio API sound synthesizer (100% code-generated chimes, arpeggios, and shockwave bass — zero external audio files required).
- **Deployment:** Vercel Global Edge Network.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/aura-spheres.git
   cd aura-spheres
   ```

2. **Open in browser:**
   Simply double-click `index.html` or serve with any static server:
   ```bash
   npx serve .
   ```
   Open `http://localhost:3000` (or `http://localhost:5173`).
